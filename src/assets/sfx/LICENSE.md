# Sample kits

The .ogg files in this directory come from the sounds of the
[uisfx](https://www.npmjs.com/package/uisfx) project, whose audio is
dedicated to the public domain under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0
Universal Public Domain Dedication. Attribution is appreciated but not
required; it is given here with thanks.

Two kits ship, a subset of the full set:

- `rubber/` - the Cartoon theme
- `scifi/`  - the Matrix theme

Every other theme's audio is synthesised at runtime by src/ui/sound.js
and involves no recordings at all.
